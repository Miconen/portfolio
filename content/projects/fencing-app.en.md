During an internship, our team of four built an app for a fencing club to manage tournaments, events and users. It went into the club's daily use.

- Round robin and bracket tournament formats
- Translations for Finnish, English, Swedish and Estonian
- Next.js and TypeScript, PostgreSQL through Kysely, tests with Vitest
- Work organised with tickets, documentation and trunk-based development

## In hindsight

I would have invested in testing earlier. We had reliability issues at first that tests would have caught. Even so, we shipped a product the client was extremely pleased with.
